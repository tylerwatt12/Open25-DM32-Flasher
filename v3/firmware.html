Diagnostic information

    

      
With sharing enabled, kd8zmm.com receives:

      
    
        
  • Your IP address, browser user agent, visit time and temporary page and operation IDs.
    • 
        
  • USB adapter IDs and connection statistics; radio chip IDs, memory sizes, boot-code fingerprint and recognition result.
    • 
        
  • Firmware version and hash, flasher version, chosen action, stages, results, error messages, codec headers and fingerprints and verification method.
    • 
      

      
Backups, filenames, radio programming, keys, calibration, raw radio data and Radio info reports are not shared. Local previews don’t send diagnostics.

      
Uncheck sharing at any time. Temporary IDs reset on each visit.

    

    

  
  
    
Important: Regulatory Compliance & Intended Use

    

      
Open25-DM32 is experimental firmware intended for amateur radio use only.

      
Transmitting with this firmware is intended exclusively for licensed amateur radio operators operating in accordance with FCC Part 97 regulations, within their authorized frequency allocations and operating privileges.

      
FCC Certification: Although the original DM-32UV hardware carries FCC Part 90 certification for certain analog FM and DMR emissions, that certification does not include P25 emissions introduced by this firmware. Open25-DM32 has not been independently tested or certified for Part 90 P25 operation.

      
Prohibited and Unsupported Uses:

      
    
        
  • Public Safety: Not intended or approved for transmitting on police, fire, EMS, government, or other Part 90 land-mobile radio systems.
    • 
        
  • Mission-Critical Communications: Do not use this firmware for life-safety, emergency response, dispatch, or any application where communication failure could result in injury, loss of life, or property damage.
    • 
        
  • Commercial Operations: Not intended for commercial, business, or other non-amateur radio communications.
    • 
        
  • Unauthorized System Access: Do not attempt to register, affiliate, or transmit on any radio system without appropriate legal authorization.
    • 
      

      
Experimental Software Disclaimer: This firmware is provided "AS IS," without warranty of any kind. It is under active development and may contain bugs, incomplete features, or unexpected behavior, including unintended RF transmissions or communication failures.

      
Users are solely responsible for ensuring their operation complies with all applicable laws, regulations, licensing requirements, and frequency restrictions.

      
This firmware is not a substitute for certified, professionally supported communications equipment.
